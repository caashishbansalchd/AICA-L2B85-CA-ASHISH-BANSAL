================================================================================
INDIAN INCOME TAX & ITR-U PROFESSIONAL CALCULATOR - OFFLINE DESKTOP BUNDLE
================================================================================

This package contains everything needed to run the Indian Income Tax & ITR-U 
Calculator completely offline on any Windows 10 or 11 computer with ZERO setup.
All prerequisite runtime software, local database, calculation engines, and
utilities are 100% self-contained inside the executable.

PREREQUISITE SOFTWARES & RUNTIME (INCLUDED & BUNDLED):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Node.js v18 LTS Runtime: Embedded directly inside IndianTaxCalculator.exe.
  You DO NOT need to install Node.js, Python, Java, or IIS.
* Embedded Web Server: High-performance local HTTP server with automatic port 
  detection (default 3000, falls back to 3001+ if in use).
* Automatic Browser Launch: Automatically opens your default web browser 
  (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave) to http://localhost:3000 upon launch.
* Zero Administrator Privileges Required: Runs cleanly from any folder, 
  Desktop, or USB flash drive without Windows UAC prompts.

BUILT-IN DATABASE & PERSISTENCE (INCLUDED & BUNDLED):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* SQLite WebAssembly (SQL.js) Database: Embedded in the offline binary.
* Client-Side Local Storage & Audit Register: Automatically saves client 
  computations, scenarios, and saved reports history with exact generation 
  timestamp.
* 100% Client-Side Privacy: All taxpayer data, PANs, income figures, and 
  records remain entirely on your local machine. No external internet or 
  cloud connection is required or used.

UTILITIES & TOOLS (INCLUDED & BUNDLED):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Professional PDF Engine: Generates formal, printable computation memos with 
  statutory verification schedules, breakdown tables, and headers.
* Excel / CSV Export: Generates downloadable spreadsheets for audit and filing.
* Advance Tax Due Dates & Schedule Engine: Computes statutory quarterly 
  installment liabilities (15 June: 15%, 15 Sept: 45%, 15 Dec: 75%, 
  15 March: 100%) and assesses Section 234C shortfall interest (1% p.m.).
* Interest Engine (Sections 234A, 234B, 234C & 220(2)): Accurate day/month 
  delay interest calculations for belated filings and advance tax defaults.
* Section 139(8A) ITR-U Module: Calculates 25% and 50% additional tax under 
  Section 140B with statutory eligibility window enforcement.
* Assessment Order Demand Calculator: Computes demand notices u/s 143(1), 
  143(3), 144, 147 with 30-day Section 220(2) interest grace periods.
* Multi-Year Tax Directory: Complete historical and future tax slabs covering 
  AY 2017-18 through AY 2031-32 (Tax Years up to 2030-31), including 
  Finance Act, 2025 Section 87A rebate rules.

PACKAGE CONTENTS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. IndianTaxCalculator.exe
   - Standalone Windows Executable (~37 MB).
   - Single-file portable executable. Double-click to launch!

2. IndianTaxCalculator.bat
   - Windows Batch Launcher fallback.
   - Launches IndianTaxCalculator.exe, or launches with system/portable Node.js.

3. build-exe.bat
   - 1-Click Recompiler script for developers wishing to rebuild the executable.

4. README-OFFLINE-DESKTOP.txt
   - This documentation file.

HOW TO RUN:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Option A: Double-click IndianTaxCalculator.exe.
Option B: Double-click IndianTaxCalculator.bat.
Option C: Open Command Prompt or PowerShell in this folder and type:
          .\IndianTaxCalculator.exe

To stop the program, simply close the console window or press Ctrl+C.
